## Risk Management & Mitigation

### 5.1 Critical Risk Factors

#### Market Risk: Competitive Response
**Risk Level**: High
**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Accelerate partner acquisition to create network effects
- Focus on specialized markets where competitors are weak
- Build customer switching costs through deep integrations
- Establish exclusive partnerships where possible

#### Operational Risk: Partner Network Development
**Risk Level**: Medium
**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Over-recruit warehouse partners (target 20+ vs minimum 15)
- Develop tier 2 and tier 3 partner options
- Create strong partner incentives and support programs
- Monitor partner satisfaction and performance closely

#### Financial Risk: Customer Acquisition Cost
**Risk Level**: Medium
**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Track CAC metrics daily and optimize spend allocation
- Focus on organic growth and referral programs
- Develop content marketing for lower-cost lead generation
- Implement conversion rate optimization across all channels

#### Technology Risk: Platform Scalability
**Risk Level**: Low
**Mitigation Strategy**:
- Conduct load testing and performance optimization
- Implement robust monitoring and alerting systems
- Develop disaster recovery and business continuity plans
- Maintain appropriate technical insurance coverage

### 5.2 Contingency Planning

#### Scenario A: Accelerated Success (25% probability)
**Triggers**: Faster customer acquisition, higher revenue
**Response**: Accelerate hiring, expand to additional markets, raise Series A early

#### Scenario B: On-Track Performance (50% probability)
**Triggers**: Meeting all KPI targets as planned
**Response**: Execute plan as designed, prepare for Series A in Month 4-5

#### Scenario C: Slower Growth (20% probability)
**Triggers**: Customer acquisition below targets, revenue shortfall
**Response**: Extend timeline, optimize marketing spend, focus on retention

#### Scenario D: Market Disruption (5% probability)
**Triggers**: Major competitor launch, economic downturn, industry disruption
**Response**: Pivot to profitable segments, reduce burn rate, extend runway
