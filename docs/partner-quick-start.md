# 🚀 Partner Quick Start Guide - Skidspace.com

## For Ebike Business Partners

### **Immediate Actions (First 30 Minutes)**
1. **Visit**: https://skidspace.com
2. **Sign Up**: Create business account
3. **Profile**: Set business type to "Electric Bicycles"
4. **Verify**: Confirm email and phone

### **Essential Setup (First Day)**
1. **Upload Logo**: Professional business branding
2. **Add Products**: 5-10 key ebike models
3. **Set Locations**: Your service areas
4. **Connect Payment**: Stripe/PayPal integration

### **Platform Basics**
- **Dashboard**: Your control center
- **Inventory**: Track all ebike stock
- **Orders**: Process customer requests
- **Warehouses**: Find storage partners
- **Analytics**: Monitor performance

### **Ebike-Specific Features**
- **Battery Storage**: Climate-controlled options
- **Safety Protocols**: Lithium battery compliance
- **Service Areas**: Workshop space booking
- **Charging Stations**: Infrastructure access

### **Getting Help**
- **Support Email**: support@skidspace.com
- **Live Chat**: Available 9am-6pm EST
- **Phone**: 1-800-SKIDSPACE
- **Training**: Schedule onboarding call

### **Success Tips**
1. **Complete Profile**: 100% profile completion = better visibility
2. **Quality Photos**: High-res ebike images boost sales
3. **Competitive Pricing**: Use market insights tools
4. **Fast Response**: Quick customer communication wins business
