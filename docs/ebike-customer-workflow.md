# 🚴‍♂️ Ebike Customer Workflow - Skidspace Platform

## Customer Journey on Skidspace.com

### **1. Customer Discovery**
- **Browse Ebikes**: Filter by type, range, price
- **Compare Models**: Side-by-side specifications
- **Read Reviews**: Customer feedback and ratings
- **Check Availability**: Real-time inventory status

### **2. Selection & Customization**
- **Choose Model**: Select preferred ebike
- **Pick Accessories**: Helmets, locks, chargers
- **Color Options**: Available variations
- **Warranty Selection**: Extended protection plans

### **3. Order Placement**
- **Cart Review**: Verify selections
- **Delivery Options**:
  - **Warehouse Pickup**: Self-collection
  - **Home Delivery**: Direct shipping
  - **Store Pickup**: Partner locations
- **Payment Processing**: Secure checkout

### **4. Order Fulfillment**
- **Order Confirmation**: Immediate email/SMS
- **Inventory Allocation**: Stock reservation
- **Preparation**: Quality check and packaging
- **Shipping**: Real-time tracking updates

### **5. Delivery & Setup**
- **Delivery Coordination**: Scheduled delivery window
- **Assembly Service**: Professional bike setup
- **Training**: Basic operation and safety
- **Registration**: Warranty activation

### **6. Post-Purchase Support**
- **Customer Portal**: Order history and support
- **Maintenance Reminders**: Service scheduling
- **Parts Ordering**: Replacement components
- **Community Access**: Rider groups and events
