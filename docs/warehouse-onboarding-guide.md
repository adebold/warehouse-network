## 🎯 Warehouse Partner Profile - Target Criteria

### **Ideal Warehouse Partner Characteristics**
- **Location**: Strategic proximity to business districts
- **Size**: 10,000+ sq ft available space
- **Security**: 24/7 monitoring & access controls
- **Technology**: Basic WMS or willingness to integrate
- **Insurance**: General liability & cargo coverage
- **References**: Proven track record with commercial clients

### **Facility Requirements**
- **Climate Control**: Temperature & humidity management
- **Loading Docks**: Multiple truck access points
- **Security Systems**: Cameras, alarms, access control
- **Fire Safety**: Sprinkler systems & safety protocols
- **Power Infrastructure**: Adequate electrical capacity
- **Internet Connectivity**: High-speed broadband

## 🚀 Warehouse Onboarding Process

### **Phase 1: Initial Contact & Qualification (Week 1)**

#### **Day 1-2: Prospecting**
- [ ] Identify target warehouses in priority markets
- [ ] Research facility capabilities & management
- [ ] Prepare customized pitch materials
- [ ] Schedule initial discovery calls

#### **Day 3-5: Discovery & Assessment**
- [ ] Conduct facility tours (virtual/in-person)
- [ ] Review technical capabilities
- [ ] Assess financial stability
- [ ] Evaluate management team
- [ ] Complete qualification scorecard

#### **Day 6-7: Proposal & Negotiation**
- [ ] Present partnership proposal
- [ ] Negotiate terms & revenue sharing
- [ ] Review legal requirements
- [ ] Establish timeline & milestones

### **Phase 2: Documentation & Legal (Week 2)**

#### **Legal Documentation**
- [ ] Warehouse partner agreement execution
- [ ] Insurance certificate verification
- [ ] Safety certification compliance
- [ ] Background checks & references
- [ ] Financial verification & credit checks

#### **Technical Assessment**
- [ ] IT infrastructure evaluation
- [ ] Integration capability assessment
- [ ] Security system review
- [ ] Capacity planning & mapping
- [ ] Operational workflow design

### **Phase 3: Platform Integration (Week 3)**

#### **Account Setup**
- [ ] Create warehouse operator admin account
- [ ] Configure facility profile in platform
- [ ] Set up payment processing & banking
- [ ] Establish inventory management system
- [ ] Configure pricing & availability rules

#### **Technical Integration**
- [ ] API key generation & testing
- [ ] WMS system integration (if applicable)
- [ ] Inventory synchronization setup
- [ ] Order management workflow
- [ ] Real-time reporting configuration

### **Phase 4: Training & Certification (Week 4)**

#### **Platform Training**
- [ ] Warehouse operator portal training
- [ ] Inventory management procedures
- [ ] Customer communication protocols
- [ ] Financial reporting & payments
- [ ] Troubleshooting & support procedures

#### **Operational Training**
- [ ] Order fulfillment workflows
- [ ] Quality control standards
- [ ] Safety protocols & procedures
- [ ] Emergency response procedures
- [ ] Customer service standards

### **Phase 5: Go-Live & Launch (Week 5-6)**

#### **Soft Launch**
- [ ] Limited customer access (internal testing)
- [ ] Process dry runs & testing
- [ ] System performance validation
- [ ] Staff readiness verification
- [ ] Issue resolution & optimization

#### **Full Launch**
- [ ] Platform listing activation
- [ ] Customer access enablement
- [ ] Marketing & promotion launch
- [ ] Performance monitoring activation
- [ ] Support system go-live

## 📋 Post-Launch Inventory Management

### **After Warehouses Are Onboarded**
Once your first 3 warehouses are operational, you'll begin managing their inventory:

#### **Inventory Categories**
- **Ebike Company**: Electric bicycles, batteries, accessories
- **General Merchandise**: Various business customer products
- **Seasonal Items**: Holiday and seasonal inventory
- **High-Value Goods**: Electronics, luxury items

#### **Inventory Input Process**
1. **Customer Onboarding**: Business customers (like ebike company)
2. **Inventory Assessment**: Catalog customer products
3. **Space Allocation**: Assign warehouse space efficiently
4. **System Setup**: Configure inventory tracking
5. **Operational Launch**: Begin storage and fulfillment

**This guide will get your first 3 warehouses successfully onboarded and ready to serve customers like your ebike company partner.**
